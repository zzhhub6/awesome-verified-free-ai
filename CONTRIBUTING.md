# Contributing & Verification Guidelines

Thank you for helping keep **awesome-verified-free-ai** accurate and free of dead promotions!

## Inclusion Criteria

1. **Direct Official Evidence Required**: Every submission or update must cite the provider's official pricing, documentation, or education/startup program URL (`https://...`). Second-hand blog posts, forum giveaways, or affiliate coupon pages are not accepted.
2. **Genuine Free Tier, Credit Grant, or Academic/Startup Program**:
   - Permanent free tiers (`$0/month` recurring allowance),
   - Free signup API credit grants,
   - Verified student/educator or startup credit programs.
   - *Excluded*: 7-day credit-card trials that auto-bill into paid subscriptions, unofficial reverse-engineered proxies, and non-AI software.
3. **Explicit Card & Limit Honesty**: State clearly whether a credit card (`card_required: yes | no | unknown`) or real-name verification is required, and note if exact quotas are console-assigned (`Tier confirmed`).

## Reporting Expired Offers

If an offer listed in `README.md` has ended or started requiring payment, open an Issue or PR moving it to [`docs/expired-graveyard.md`](./docs/expired-graveyard.md) with the official announcement or pricing link.
